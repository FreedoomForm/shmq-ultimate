# v260 deep research: restore v258 geometry and remove redundant persistent-tensor stream records

The v259 T4 result proves that the valid 8-warp 32x128 native pair is still slower than the accepted staged overlap: Qwen mixed rows=128 fell from v258 `0.373x` to `0.193x`, while timing-integrity rose to `2.172x`. Therefore v259's mixed native-pair selector and geometry are rejected and must be rolled back; the v258 FP16 output ABI remains the performance baseline.

A second source comparison focused on allocator bookkeeping. The upstream MixLLM launcher keeps immutable packed weights, scales, zeros, indices, and kernel configuration in persistent module/launcher state. SHMQ's `run_int4_pair_partition` records seven tensors on every auxiliary-stream launch, and `run_cutlass_int_partition` records seven more. The input quantization tensor, activation scale tensor, output tensor, and temporary transposed CUTLASS metadata still require per-call lifetime protection. The module-owned weight/scale/zero/index buffers and the module-owned expanded INT4 cache are kept alive by the Python module itself throughout the forward call and do not require `recordStream` to prevent allocator reuse.

v260 therefore restores the v258 staged mixed path and removes only redundant `record_tensor_stream` calls for persistent module-owned operands. It retains recording for dynamic activations/output and for temporary matrix metadata. No tensor is freed, no computation is removed, no benchmark setting changes, and no quality/tolerance changes are made. The change is expected to reduce launcher bookkeeping modestly; it is not claimed to solve the fundamental large-M staged SM75 deficit unless Kaggle gates prove it.
